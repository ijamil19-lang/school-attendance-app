Monthly Student Attendance Summary

Attendance percentage = (Present + Late + Left Early) ÷ recorded attendance days × 100. Excused days are excluded from the denominator. Unrecorded school days are not counted.

Students Needing Attention

Based on recorded attendance for the selected month. Low attendance: below 75%; frequent absence: 3 or more absent days; repeated lateness: 3 or more late days. These indicators do not imply a reason for absence.

School grading rules (admin)

One grade per line in the format A:80. Example only: A:80, B:70, C:60, D:50, F:0. Each school chooses its own boundaries. Include 0%.
